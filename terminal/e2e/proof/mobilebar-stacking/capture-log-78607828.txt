=== Rename/edit disclosure (MAJOR-1 repair) ===
The tool's own filename convention is `<label>-<viewport>.<ext>` and
`<label>.json` (see measure-analysis-mobilebar-stacking.mjs:213,231,240),
where `<label>` is whatever free-text tag is passed as argv[2] — here
`raw-before-r12` / `raw-after-r12` (the CODE_HEAD sha is not known to the
caller until the run is captured, and is only knowable as the JSON's own
`capturedAtHead` field, per the tool's own header comment). The tool
therefore wrote:
  /tmp/proof-r12/raw-before-r12-1440x900.png, raw-before-r12-390x844.png, raw-before-r12.json
  /tmp/proof-r12/raw-after-r12-1440x900.png,  raw-after-r12-390x844.png,  raw-after-r12.json
Those 6 files were then copied into terminal/e2e/proof/mobilebar-stacking/ and:
  - the 4 PNGs were renamed, byte-for-byte, with NO content change, to the
    ruling's `<viewport>-<before|after>-<CODE_HEAD8>.png` form (verified
    below by SHA-256 — the committed bytes are identical to the tool's own
    output bytes, not re-encoded or re-touched).
  - the 2 JSONs had exactly two string fields hand-edited post-capture: the
    top-level `label` (`raw-before-r12` -> `before-78607828`, and
    `raw-after-r12` -> `after-78607828`) and, per viewport, the `screenshot`
    field (renamed to match the renamed PNG, e.g.
    `raw-before-r12-1440x900.png` -> `1440x900-before-78607828.png`). NO
    other field in either JSON was touched — `capturedAtHead`, `port`, and
    every measurement value (`mobilebar`, `mobilebarZIndexCascade`,
    `finPane`, `finHead`, `menuButton`, `menuHitTest`,
    `mobilebarOverlapsFinPane`, `finHeadCoveredByMobilebar`) are exactly the
    tool's own raw output. This is provable without trusting this sentence:
    the tool's raw, unedited stdout for both runs is appended verbatim below
    (RAW TOOL STDOUT section) — diff it against the committed
    measurement-{before,after}-78607828.json and the only differences are
    the two renamed fields per file.
