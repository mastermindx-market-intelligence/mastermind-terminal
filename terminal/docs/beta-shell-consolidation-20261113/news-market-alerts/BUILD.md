# News Intelligence and Market Alerts
## Shared Shell design and engineering handoff · 14 November 2026

**Design status.** Twenty-four source layouts cover twelve News and twelve Alert states. The PDF contains the complete set, with bookmarks. The ZIP includes one fixed-size HTML layer composition per board and a manifest. The separate HTML prototype supports navigation, selection, simulated source returns, saved references, staged filters and coverage states. The layer compositions are editable authoring inputs. Paper has not returned a new page, artboard ID, screenshot or JSX. Validate the compositions inside Paper before production implementation.

**Source status.** News uses an explicitly fictional issuer, Northline, and invented publishers, text and clocks. The corrected $500m to $50m maximum over five years follows section 6.3 of the house masterplan. The alert examples come from four records in the retained November 14 Macro publication. The design does not use current market data. Partial, quiet, entitlement, delay and outage examples are simulations.

### 1. User journeys and board map

| Boards | Entry and decision | What the reader can do | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| N01, N06 | Open the newsroom on desktop or phone. Decide whether a material correction merits investigation. | Read the changed claim and compare original vs corrected scale. Open **Inspect correction**, or continue down the ordinary stream. | One lead, same story excluded from the following list. The source and reporting status remain visible. One primary action before the first scroll. |
| N02, N03 | Establish the amount, source and remaining uncertainty. | Compare original and amended wording. Inspect primary notice, original wire and syndicated repeat. Return to the originating report. | Three publications trace to two origins. The ceiling never becomes booked revenue. Every copy has a publisher and a role. Use previous wording only while rights permit. |
| N04, N08, N09, N12 | Return to a saved finding after the underlying source changes. | Preserve the original revision reference, discover a newer revision, and group references into a local investigation. | Save stores review identity only. Withdraw removes protected text from all surfaces immediately. An original revision may be unavailable. Saved collections never assert cloud sync. |
| N05, N07, N10, N11 | Scan an active feed, narrow scope and resume it later. | Buffer ordinary arrivals, save a view, edit followed topics, stage a phone filter and Apply or Cancel. | Arrivals preserve selection and the scroll anchor. A correction or withdrawal applies even while arrivals are held. Cancel leaves the current filters untouched. A zero-result filter offers clear/reset. |
| A01, A06 | Open the shared Market Alerts desk. Find the first dated observation to investigate. | Open the radar transition or scan Watch next, For awareness and Earlier priority. | Preserve source tier, event age and display eligibility. A stale critical signal never fills the fresh lead slot. No cards are padded. |
| A02, A04 | Understand why the radar observation appears. | Inspect the previous state, observed state, both warnings as a count, source day and optional priority breakdown. | Only display named warning details if supplied by the source. Attention 90 is separate from the recorded market condition. |
| A03, A09, A10 | Investigate recurrence without inventing a continuous state. | Read the actual first/latest dates, the reported total and gap; explore dated source firings where the history owner supports them. | Four observed copper firings over 24 days; Oct 16 and Nov 9 are dated. Do not place invented intermediate ticks on a calendar. Expose truncated history and its scope. |
| A05, A11 | Find an observation and compare its original meaning with the current source panel. | Filter, inspect original evidence, follow an exact source anchor and return with selected ID, tab, filters and focus intact. | Selecting a newer read does not change the old event. A missing/undated current read never substitutes an original number. |
| A07, A08, A12 | Distinguish a quiet board from missing evidence. | See healthy quiet, inspect retained history, or identify the failed source while continuing through surviving observations. | Failure removes affected totals and whole-market synthesis. A quiet claim requires successful, entitled coverage. The private watchlist producer contributes neither rows nor totals to the shared view. |

Stand-alone prototype: use **Design preview** at the lower right to choose a desk and scenario. **Browse desks** in the rail or header switches between desks. Open the corrected filing, inspect Sources, read the republished wire and return. Use **Add to investigation** from the story reader. In Alerts, inspect the regime observation, open **Why here?**, return, then open copper and **Firing history**. Demo source-destination screens intentionally identify the retained/current boundary. The PDF is the visual authoring reference. The prototype exercises behavior with a compact specimen set, including retained history and source-coverage simulations.

### 2. Layout and interaction contract

- Use the existing Terminal AppShell, shell toolbar, compact/expanded rail and mobile navigation. The 72px compact rail and 64px desktop header match the shared shell; the 52px phone header is the design target. The existing Browse directory admits these desks. Do not add permanent News or Market Alerts buttons to the global rail. The desktop specimen assumes compact chrome; the page must respond to the actual content width when the expanded 224px rail is selected.
- Use the incumbent shell theme tokens, presentation preferences, market selector and translation owner. This design proposes editorial serif headlines in the desk content, an off-white reading surface, dark green lead with high-contrast type, numeric comparison labels, and neutral row dividers. Map them to semantic tokens in each supported theme. Global chrome continues to use shell typography. Green on the lead identifies the selected editorial treatment; it conveys no bullish judgment.
- On desktop, use one open reading area. Lead and stream do not repeat the same headline/summary. Compare is a reader action. Methodology, score components, raw clocks and source inventory live one level deeper. Full-width paired evidence gives way to stacked labeled pairs at narrow widths or large text. Mobile opens the reader in the available page width, provides real Back and restores the trigger.
- Show the first meaningful observation before the first mobile fold at 390 × 844 and 320px width with ordinary text. At 200% zoom or 200% text, stack and scroll vertically, preserve controls and full meaning. Keyboard focus must remain visible, modals must suspend the background, Escape returns to the correct parent. Use 44px minimum touch targets in the implementation even where the outlined control is shorter. Respect reduced motion. Do not rely on red/green, strikethrough or line length alone to communicate the correction. Use an accessible text pair with amounts, units, source and time.
- Workspace URL owns acknowledged market, desk tab, filter state, selection, source membership and reader tab. Keep anchor/scroll/focus in navigation state. Back from a source must restore the exact row. Refresh and copied links restore the selected ID if it remains entitled; a lost selection uses explicit unavailable UI. A direct link with no originating desk falls back to the canonical desk. Closed-domain, same-origin return targets only.
- Existing exact shell search, profile, ticker, language and presentation controls stay with their original owners. The preview implements desk interactions only. In production, Recently viewed, unread markers and followed topic preferences must keep their established scope. The interface never treats viewing a source as saving, acknowledging or delivering an alert.

### 3. Source-to-screen contracts

| Need | Reuse and required behavior |
|---|---|
| Market News summary | Macro's press daemon and IntelligenceStore remain aggregate publishing owners. The incumbent intelligence and wires artifacts have independent schemas and update clocks. Read the aggregate through the protected snapshot/coverage contract; keep publication, event, revision, fetch and heartbeat time distinct. A transport reconnect must not make an old report fresh. The request to move engines brings their existing output into the shell rather than starting a second producer. |
| News reader, SSE and history | Candidate Macro #9612 carries app/news_desk.py and the authenticated /api/news-desk/{snapshot,stream,coverage,history} family. Confirm merged/deployed availability and entitlement before wiring. Bind canonical story ID, report/member identity, revision, origin group, outlet, transport, rights, timestamp precision, language and retained change status to the actual candidate schema. No inferred independence from publication count. Reconnect with the existing cursor/sequence contract; reconcile a source snapshot on expiry, duplicates and gaps. Keep buffered arrivals separate from correction/removal messages. |
| Company handoff | Reuse Terminal /api/news/[symbol], /api/news/stories/[storyId], /api/news/stream and its TickerNewsPanel/feed/validation code, backed by the existing qbus ticker writer. Match an exact security/listing before enabling a handoff. Do not feed the derived macro-desk JSON into the ticker ingestion system. If no exact security is resolved, retain the story and hide the ticker handoff. Preserve the full-market search independently of the company workspace. |
| Access, retention and briefs | Reuse server-side auth, entitlement, allowlisted upstream origin, rights filtering and per-account cache boundaries. Public shell with premium payload requires authorized server reads. A shareable URL is never a bearer token. At withdrawal, purge protected text including stale cache, compare panes and saved previews. Persist only permitted references, read markers and view/query preferences. No account-scoped claim from browser-local state. Separate excerpts and original reporting from attributed analysis. AI briefs require their own rights, revision, validation and cost owners. |
| Alert triage | Macro engine/alert_triage.py and engine/alert_time.py own alert_id, tier, severity, priority/components, source, event day/instant, source-as-of, date precision, board timezone, first/last event, firings, coverage and validation. A pure presenter consumes this projection. Macro #7022 engine/alert_center_view.py is a candidate for the existing attention/explorer behavior. Do not recalculate scores in React. If only a 60-row capped snapshot is reachable, label that window explicitly. Do not claim a complete archive. |
| Event vs. source | Resolve allowed source anchors through the existing fixed dashboard BFF or approved product route, with original alert_id + event day and validated return. Shared Shell Macro Dashboard #1503 is adjacent context, a subset of the desk. Date-only records retain date precision. A missing previous value or current timestamp is unavailable. Same subject or repeated source does not establish independence. A family scorecard is scoped to its own measurement, not an individual signal's probability. |
| Private monitors | Terminal /alerts keeps user conditions, dry runs, receipts, permissions and delivery. Monitor #1512 owns private retained evidence. The market desk has no acknowledge/archive/delivery write. A future explicit handoff to a supported private rule would have to use that owner's form and confirmation. |

### 5. Acceptance tests and next canvas work

| Case | Required result in the actual Terminal consumer |
|---|---|
| New event arrives while reader has a source selected | Unrelated arrivals buffer. The selected revision changes immediately on correction; withdrawal removes protected content on every open/saved surface. No focus jump or duplicate group on reconnect. |
| A copied article arrives via another transport | Source-group count and member count remain distinguishable. The syndicated member links to its origin. A missing origin stays unknown. |
| Clock precision or market entitlement changes | Preserve original precision/timezone, filter invalid selections, withhold unentitled text from markup/cache. Offer a real scope-appropriate next action. |
| Private sources, partial coverage, healthy zero | No private data in public/shared counts, payloads or aggregates. Quiet only after consequential sources report. Surviving rows still open. |
| Touch/keyboard, light/dark, EN/ZH | No horizontal page scroll or clipped actions at supported widths, long names wrap, escape/Back restores row and focus. Search and filters are navigable without a pointer. Use visual and assistive-technology checks, including actual 320px and 200% zoom. |
| Current source panel fails or alert has missing history | Original observation remains readable; no inferred current reading, intermediate timestamp, continuous condition or trading action. Preserve the cursor and exact source link. |
| Existing private delivery and regional desks | /alerts continues to manage private alert rules. Opening a shared alert performs no personal mutation. Regional routes retain their original producers. |
