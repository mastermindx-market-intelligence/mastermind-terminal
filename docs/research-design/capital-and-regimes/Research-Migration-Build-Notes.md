## Eight workflows and their engine contracts

### 1. WTI Live Path

**Investor question.** Has the oil shock progressed into inflation, real yields and long-duration equity weakness? The overview names the first failed condition. Selecting a station changes the reading, threshold ruler and evidence. The private assumption drawer accepts a hypothetical oil return and evaluates it with the frozen positive moving-average slope. Closing the drawer returns to the observed path.

**Contract.** Use authenticated GET /api/ontology/explorer/v1 and its snapshot revision. Retain ordered legs, effective date, first-known cutoff, data source and observation windows. The archived 25 September example has +17.783% versus a strict >25% and positive 50-day slope, +2 bp versus >15 bp over 22 sessions, +65 bp over 63 sessions and −0.59 pp QQQ versus SPY over 63 sessions. The first two legs fail. The last two meet their local conditions independently. Price data was observed on 25 September and built on 28 September. No validated historical baseline accompanies that example.

**Build and prove.** Mount the owner response in the shell. Preserve the existing Company/Thesis return context and focus. Check actual entitlement before loading readings. Explicit holdings lookup is a separate user action in the incumbent portfolio context. Carry owner-issued validity and signed-in access across that return. Proof includes strict equality at +25%, missing comparison, sign-out while evidence is open, disconnected scenario vs observed state and no private context in a Thesis payload. ### 8. Stock Seasonality

**Investor question.** How often did a calendar window recur, and does a small date change overturn the evidence? The SPY fan rebases each of 25 complete years to zero at the chosen start. A second, collapsed chart shows those years from January with the window shaded. Select a year, then move a start or end date to recompute every path, the median and up-year count. The custom window becomes exploratory. Full-year context is sampled weekly in this study. The detail fan uses all available daily slots within its bounded date controls.

**Contract and proof.** Decode the source’s scaled cumulative log returns. For each year use exp(logValue[end] − logValue[start]) − 1. The 11 October–10 December archive produces 22 positive years out of 25 and +5.3% median. SPY uses itself as benchmark. For other supported symbols preserve raw and market-neutral results, their own null distributions and window stability. Keep the incomplete current year outside historical denominators. Price revision vintage is distinct from point-in-time adjustment. Non-SPY entity fetch failure preserves the former graph and its name together.

**Next boards.** Full-range date handles, symbol picker, whole-year interaction, independently qualified neutral series, empty/ineligible symbol and failed entity switch. Preserve the existing Terminal seasonal controls for keyboard year selection and focus restoration. Do not expose a catalyst explanation until its owner supplies one.
