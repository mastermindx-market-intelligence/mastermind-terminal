A repeatable practice
Review the decision.
Then reveal the result.
A profitable trade can break your process. A loss can follow a sound plan. Neither single outcome proves skill or luck.
Before looking at profit or loss
Ask three questions.
Was the original view supported by the information available then?
Did your action respect the entry condition, horizon and falsifiers you wrote down?
Did you change the plan because evidence changed, or because the outcome felt uncomfortable?
