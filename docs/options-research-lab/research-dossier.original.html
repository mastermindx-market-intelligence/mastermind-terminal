Mastermind Terminal — 3D Options Research Lab

Research, product design and implementation direction

Research date: October 7, 2026 · Design release R1 · Not a live trading product

Delivery state. Seven original editable Paper artboards have been created in the existing Terminal design file. They specify five connected research lenses, a mobile exact-slice workflow, and light-theme source/unavailable states. Numerical examples are synthetic illustrations, not market observations or execution recommendations. Application implementation, production data admission, browser performance, empirical signal validation and deployment remain unfinished.

Paper: https://app.paper.design/file/01M3NSZGE8JWN2EZCSF0DT05RM/p-H-0

1. Product decision

Build a research workspace, not an isolated 3D options widget. Its central promise is: locate a structure, understand what changed, inspect the evidence, and stress a possible expression without losing context. The same instrument, exact contract identities, selected observation and provenance must follow the researcher across Chain, Volatility, Replay, Flow and Scenario lenses.

Three approaches were considered. A direct reproduction of the screenshots would be visually attractive but would inherit ambiguous gamma signs, large overlapping bubbles and weak historical context. A replacement options platform would offer control but duplicate Terminal’s existing options, replay, flow and planning owners. The chosen approach is a thin, linked 3D investigation layer over those owners, with exact 2D slices and an evidence inspector.

The default layout is deliberately calm: instrument and clocks, a one-sentence research brief, the primary visualization, a pinned inspector and a precision strip. Advanced filters, units, model definitions and raw observations are progressively disclosed. The application should explain which feature merits investigation; it should not force the user to decode every node unaided.

This is not a claim that 3D is inherently more accurate. A published visualization study found important disadvantages for interactive 3D on its tested cluster-separation tasks. That finding is task-specific, but it argues for measuring our own tasks and retaining a strong 2D route rather than relying on visual novelty. [S10]

Professional workflow benchmark

Cboe’s Mosaic describes visual chain summaries connected to detailed inspection, volatility surfaces, term structure and activity comparisons. The relevant benchmark is a fast transition from overview to exact evidence—not copying its interface or accepting its marketing claims as validation. [S17]

Cboe DataShop separately describes delta-relative and price-relative volatility surfaces, at constant maturities or listed expiries. That distinction reinforces our explicit coordinate/tenor controls; those datasets are not assumed available under Mastermind’s present entitlements. [S18]

Mastermind’s proposed differentiation is the continuous evidence path through package uncertainty, dual-clock replay, source capability states and manually defined scenarios. We have not benchmarked its runtime performance or predictive accuracy against a professional product.

2. What the supplied examples actually encode

The screenshots place strike on the vertical axis, expiry on depth, and a selected metric on the horizontal axis. Calls and puts are separated visually. A spot reference crosses the scene; bubbles show traded volume and outer rings show open interest. The horizontal metric can be price, change from prior close, implied volatility or gamma. Selection reveals a contract card. A separate time mode appears intended to show recorded history.

Those are useful building blocks, not a complete intelligence system. Our design preserves the landscape, fast switching and contract selection while making every dimension explicit. Expiry depth must not silently become observation-time depth. Missing observations must not become zero. Perspective must not make two equal-size observations look materially different merely because one is further away.

Several financial interpretations require correction:

Volume versus open interest. Volume counts trading activity; open interest counts outstanding contracts. A transaction can increase, decrease or leave open interest unchanged depending on both counterparties’ opening/closing status. Consequently, volume exceeding prior open interest is a turnover clue—not proof of fresh directional positioning. [S2]

Gamma sign. Long calls and long puts have positive gamma; short positions have negative gamma. Gamma measures the local change in delta for an underlying-price change. A chart that puts puts to the left and calls to the right may be a useful layout convention, but the left/right position is not evidence of dealer inventory sign. [S1]

Exercise and settlement. SPY options and SPX options cannot be treated as interchangeable merely because both reference the S&P 500 ecosystem. Cboe distinguishes physical/American-style SPY options from cash/European-style SPX options. Settlement and exercise information therefore belongs in contract identity and model admission, not a footnote. [S3]

The examples’ zero-rate, zero-dividend European approximation must not become an unlabeled production valuation engine. Vendor Greek conventions also matter: ThetaData’s documentation distinguishes model choices, time handling and Greek scaling. Its documentation is evidence about that provider, not proof that every Terminal record was generated with those assumptions. [S4]

3. The five connected lenses

3.1 Chain landscape: locate concentration and unusual activity

The primary board uses strike × expiry × session volume, with calls and puts mirrored solely for separation. The volume dot and prior-OI ring use a common area scale. The selected 785 call shows 243,000 volume, 110,000 prior OI and a 2.21× turnover ratio; unknown quotes and unreported next-session ΔOI stay explicitly unavailable.

The production metric selector should include session volume, interval volume, prior OI, published ΔOI, producer-supplied unusual-volume measures, contract prices, IV and Greeks, with capability gating. Aggregate exposure is a separate family from per-contract Greeks. Controls expose side, strike/moneyness window, exact expiry, tenor group, liquidity conditions and admissible-source status.

Click pins one contract; shift-click or an explicit Compare action adds up to three. The exact strip switches between a strike slice at one expiry, an expiry slice at one strike and a sortable table. Selection must survive camera rotation and lens changes. A filter that excludes a pinned contract shows “outside current filter” rather than silently choosing another.

The horizontal quantity uses a linear scale by default. Very heavy tails may justify a named log/symlog transform, but ticks must show actual units and the comparison strip must stay exact. Never apply a silent radius cap that makes several different volumes look equal. If markers are visually capped, show that fact and retain uncapped values in selection.

A gamma variant of this lens uses positive per-share contract gamma for long-option conventions, with side separation labeled. An exposure variant uses the producer’s actual exposure units and inventory assumptions. These variants share the same chart architecture; they are not a license to infer absent fields from aggregate GEX.

3.2 Volatility terrain: understand relative pricing and shape

The second board uses forward log-moneyness, tenor and implied volatility. Raw observations, an admitted fitted surface and excluded regions remain distinguishable. The selected synthetic point has 18.2% fitted IV at log(K/F)=+0.02 and 18 calendar days, versus a matched 17.2% reference. A linked 2D slice makes the one-volatility-point difference legible.

For research, compare like with like: the same forward-moneyness coordinate, tenor convention, model family and observation context. Absolute IV, total variance and changes are separate views. Strike, spot moneyness and delta-coordinate views may also be offered, but the transformation and forward source must be inspectable.

A smooth mesh is not evidence of reliable prices. A production fitted surface requires quote-quality screening, inversion checks, bid–ask residual reporting, coverage limits and static-arbitrage admission. Gatheral and Jacquier provide a research basis for arbitrage-free SVI/SSVI construction; adopting a family would still require calibration and empirical tests on our data. [S8]

Useful overlays include event expiries, historical matched-tenor bands, current/reference differences, risk reversals, butterflies and forward variance. None of these should be sold as expected returns. The visualization consumer should not run an independent browser fitter; fitting and valuation remain with the established model producer.

3.3 Replay and change: isolate when a structure emerged

The replay board fixes an expiry and replaces expiry depth with observation time. Its selected five-minute interval contributes 42,750 contracts, while cumulative volume moves from 200,250 to 243,000. These two representations are intentionally separate. A missing frame is visible rather than smoothed into an apparently continuous event.

Every record needs both the market observation time and the time it became available to the system. The design illustrates a frame observed at 14:30:00 but available at 14:30:08. An observation-time view can display that delay; a knowledge-time replay with a 14:30:00 cutoff must exclude the frame.

This is a proposed point-in-time contract, not a claim that all current archives satisfy it. Historical OI must also respect publication availability. ThetaData documents its own prior-day OI update behavior; that schedule does not substitute for recording the availability of the actual consumed record. [S5]

A/B comparison freezes camera, scale, universe and filters. It reports retained, entered, exited and missing contracts separately. “Change” should not accidentally mean that different contracts were included. Corrections need their own revision lineage. Missing interval endpoints, counter resets and canceled trades should prevent an invented interval-volume result.

3.4 Flow and packages: separate prints from inferred structure

The flow board plots execution time × strike × expiry; area represents contracts and hue represents call/put type—not aggressor direction. Candidate links distinguish likely related legs from confirmed exchange/package relationships.

The synthetic example is two 500-contract calls at 785 and 790, executed four milliseconds apart at $0.48 and $0.18. Their gross leg premium is $33,000 at the stated 100-share multiplier. The design does not declare a verified net debit or the identity of the opening participant.

The evidence panel shows trade conditions, original/revised state, prior bid/ask, quote age, execution timing and association rationale. ThetaData’s trade/quote endpoint documents quote pairing at or before trade time, with a strict-prior option. This is a concrete starting point for adapter tests, not an assurance that quote-location classification is always correct. [S6]

Verified complex-order net price is a different object from summing the absolute premiums of independent legs. Cboe’s complex-order specification explicitly treats net price at the package level. [S9] Without a reliable package identifier, related timing, size and strikes support a candidate association; they do not erase alternatives such as unrelated trades.

The “model a hypothetical orientation” action asks the researcher to choose legs and directions. It does not turn a possible association into an actual position or executable ticket.

3.5 Scenario lab: stress payoff, timing and execution assumptions

The first scenario board is deliberately mathematically exact at expiration: a manually specified 785/790 debit call spread, one unit, a $0.30 per-share debit and a 100-share multiplier. Before costs, its expiration loss is bounded at $30, profit at $470 and break-even at $785.30. At an expiration underlying price of $787, the payoff is +$170.

The third dimension is entry debit, showing how fill assumptions change the outcome. This is more useful than using a polished pre-expiry surface generated from an unidentified model. The same lab can later show underlying price × volatility shock × modeled P&L, or price × time × P&L, only when the existing valuation owner supplies an admitted model and assumptions.

Scenarios should include spread costs, slippage, residual legs, financing assumptions, exercise/assignment context and alternative volatility-response conventions. An expiration payoff diagram does not capture all operational risks. Physical settlement can leave an underlying position, which is materially different from cash settlement. [S11]

No probability is implied by the surface. A future probability overlay would require a separately identified measure and calibrated distribution. A user-entered scenario quantity is not a position-size recommendation.

4. Units, definitions and model boundaries

The following equations define proposed product quantities; they do not assert current backend coverage.

For a standard European continuous-yield Black–Scholes illustration,

d1 = [ln(S/K) + (r − q + σ²/2)T] / (σ√T)

Γ = exp(−qT) φ(d1) / (S σ √T)

where σ is a decimal volatility and T is a year fraction. This formula is a reference case, not a production default for American exercise, discrete dividends or near-expiry edge conditions. The derivatives must come from the admitted model. [S16]

With per-share gamma Γ, signed contract position N and contract multiplier M, a local approximation to the change in delta-equivalent shares from a $1 underlying move is Γ N M. The approximate dollar value of the incremental hedge shares for a 1% underlying move is:

GEX_1pct = Γ × N × M × S² × 0.01

That is not the option’s gamma P&L. Local curvature P&L is approximately 0.5 × Γ × N × M × (ΔS)². The distinction follows by differentiating delta and applying the quadratic price expansion. Exposure definitions from existing producers must be preserved or explicitly converted, never guessed from a “GEX” label.

OI can weight an unsigned activity/exposure proxy, but it does not supply signed dealer N. The product should offer “observed quantity,” “producer proxy” and “assumed inventory scenario” as visibly different modes. Recalculating a gamma ridge under a spot shock requires a new model evaluation; multiplying the old gamma by a new spot is only a local approximation.

Every Greek metric needs a unit descriptor. Vega may be per unit decimal volatility or per one percentage point. Theta and charm depend on the time variable and sign convention. Vanna must identify its volatility unit. Preserve contract multiplier and deliverable; do not silently assume every adjusted contract represents 100 identical shares.

For volatility research, k=ln(K/F) and w(k,T)=σ(k,T)²T. At fixed k, the implied forward variance over two tenors is [w(k,T2)−w(k,T1)]/(T2−T1). A negative result from a purported admitted surface should trigger inspection, not become an attractive-looking trade recommendation. Different event exposures and strike mappings must be retained.

“Move today” should distinguish changes in bid, ask, midpoint, last trade and an official close. Percentage change from a tiny or invalid denominator is unstable by arithmetic. Pair it with absolute change, denominator, age and quote-quality status; do not let a stale last print produce the leading research headline.

5. Features that make this genuinely more useful

The following are original proposed research capabilities, not validated alpha claims.

Activity persistence. Track whether a same-session turnover anomaly is followed by a later published increase in OI, while retaining the interval of uncertainty and possible rolls. Compare adjacent strikes and expiries. The output is “activity persisted into the next clearing observation,” not “these prints were all opening buys.”

Volatility shock propagation. Detect where matched-moneyness/tenor residuals change first, then whether the change spreads across expiries or related instruments. Control for the underlying move and scheduled events. This can expose whether a visibly large print coincided with repricing or merely crossed an already-wide market.

Gamma-ridge migration. Show how an admitted modeled exposure surface changes under spot, time and inventory-sign scenarios. Compare the location and concentration of peaks, not just their current height. Display sensitivity to the sign assumption; do not present a single proxy-based “dealer wall” as observed inventory.

Package-aware investigation. Keep linked structures intact when summarizing flow, avoid counting every leg as independent conviction, and expose alternative orientations. A calendar roll may relocate risk through time without being a simple bullish call purchase.

Liquidity-adjusted anomaly triage. Rank research candidates using transparent components: time-of-day activity surprise, dollar amount, quoted spread, quote age, contract coverage, neighbor consistency and sample count. OI/volume alone should not be treated as guaranteed liquidity. [S2] Prefer component explanations over an opaque “conviction 97” badge.

Event and cross-asset comparison. Compare analogous tenor/moneyness regions around earnings, macro releases or sector events. Use the existing event and market-ontology owners. Separate index, ETF and single-name exercise/settlement characteristics. Normalization aids comparison but does not make those instruments interchangeable.

Evidence-bound assistant. Questions such as “What changed around the 785 calls?” should resolve to the current selection, metrics, snapshot references, clocks and model versions. Answers separate observations, interpretations, counter-evidence and the next discriminating check. A missing source stays missing. Saving an investigation saves evidence references and assumptions through the existing owner, not an ungrounded chat summary.

Useful saved recipes include near-expiry activity acceleration, skew/term repricing, neighboring-strike comparison, likely-roll investigation and spread-fill stress. The recipe selects a view and evidence checklist; it does not create a second signal publisher.

6. Data and architecture findings from Terminal

This census used mastermindx-market-intelligence/mastermind-terminal at commit ad36a332cd4b53af1d917a94f6fb3a10e27dad84. It is pinned source evidence, not a production-state assertion. Exact paths and limits are recorded in the implementation handoff.

The existing matrix contract contains strike/expiry cells, GEX, call/put OI and volume, optional ΔOI and unusual-activity metadata. It does not contain the per-contract quote, individual Greek and complete valuation metadata required for every proposed lens. The honest first slice therefore uses existing activity/positioning quantities; enrichment comes through established data owners.

Terminal already contains Surface/Replay, GEX, volatility, flow cache/stream and OptionsHub integration points. Relevant payoff and volatility work existed on separate pull requests at the census boundary. Those carriers must be reconciled rather than overwritten. Macro’s options-intelligence program also owns broader research/producer decisions; this proposal is a consumer expansion, not a replacement program.

The preferred client implementation is TypeScript/React with a lazy-loaded Three.js scene, optionally using React Three Fiber after compatibility checks. Three.js supports instanced meshes and instance-aware picking. Those capabilities support many selectable marks without a separate React element per contract. [S12, S13]

Keep state changes outside the render loop where possible, reuse buffers, bound data windows, stop animation when idle and dispose resources on unmount. React Three Fiber’s guidance supports demand-driven rendering and avoiding repeated state updates in tight rendering paths. [S14, S15] These are implementation choices—not measured performance results.

No new independent polling loop, price model, replay archive, authentication plane, worker queue or AI grounding system is required. The scene consumes normalized records and emits selections. Existing owners retain fetching, caching, provenance, valuation, historical availability and saved-investigation persistence.

7. Usability, responsiveness and accessibility

The desktop research area is 1,016 pixels wide with a 350-pixel inspector inside a 1,440-pixel board. A normal application viewport must scroll or reflow shorter vertical space rather than clip the lower evidence strip. The 390-pixel mobile board defaults to an exact expiry slice and a contract sheet; 3D remains available, not mandatory. A light tablet board demonstrates source and capability inspection.

Camera presets are front, top and an orthographic research perspective. Rotate, zoom, reset, keyboard selection, exact tables and reduced-motion mode must all work. Hold a camera when comparing snapshots. Labels should orient toward the reader; marker ordering must not hide the pinned point. Overlap needs a disambiguation picker rather than random selection.

Colour distinguishes type or a stated scalar, not several unrelated meanings at once. Calls use observation blue and puts orange in this design; direction uses separate explicit labels. Colour is supplemented with text and selected-state outlines. Tooltip content moves to a pinned panel so it never obscures the only point being investigated.

Missing metrics, stale observations, missing replay frames, inadequate entitlements, invalid fits and GPU unavailability have different messages and next actions. A failed 3D renderer falls back to exact slices without losing the selected instrument. Loading shows the currently known coverage, not a fabricated complete universe.

The seven artboards are a buildable direction, not proof of all responsive states. Both application themes, Chinese localization, 200% text sizing, keyboard behavior and browser rendering remain acceptance obligations.

8. Validation and release criteria

First validate semantics: finite inputs, null-versus-zero distinctions, units, contract identities, product settlement, prior-OI availability, quote alignment, correction handling and model version compatibility. Then validate interaction parity between 3D selection, 2D slices, tables and saved evidence.

Numerical fixtures in this delivery check only the synthetic examples and reference arithmetic. They do not validate an application, vendor Greek implementation, fitted surface or trading signal. The synthetic volatility polynomial is an illustration and has not been admitted as an arbitrage-free production surface.

For performance, propose test cases at 1,000, 5,000 and 20,000 points, with scene construction, pointer-picking latency, frame time, idle CPU, memory, network duplication and mobile fallback recorded. Initial targets might be sub-100 ms selection response and smooth 30 fps interaction on the agreed baseline hardware. These are proposed budgets to qualify, not achieved measurements.

For intelligence claims, use point-in-time availability, matched time-of-day/tenor/moneyness controls, correction-aware historical records, relevant corporate actions and held-out dates/instruments. Compare against simple baselines before adding model complexity. Include transaction-cost sensitivity and multiple-hypothesis controls. Report sample size, uncertainty and failure regimes rather than a single success rate.

Release is incremental: evidence/selection foundation; chain visualization; admitted quote/Greek enrichment; volatility/flow/replay connections; scenarios and saved investigations; then measured production qualification. A merged branch or passing synthetic fixture alone does not establish live data correctness or usefulness.

Current next step: obtain a genuinely cleared host execution preflight, create one isolated implementation carrier, reconcile existing payoff/volatility/replay work, and implement the first activity landscape with exact source parity. The requested end-to-end mission remains open.

A selection outside the current filter is identified as such. A root or identity change invalidates incompatible detail rather than reusing a stale tooltip. Opening the original prints carries exact contract/time references, not just a ticker. Opening Plan creates a manually chosen hypothesis, not an inferred fill. Saving follows the existing investigation owner.

Camera controls: orthographic default, named front/top presets, orbit, zoom and reset. Axis transforms and marker caps are named and reversible. Comparison locks scales. Exact values remain in the inspector/table; no screen-coordinate inversion is the source of financial values.
