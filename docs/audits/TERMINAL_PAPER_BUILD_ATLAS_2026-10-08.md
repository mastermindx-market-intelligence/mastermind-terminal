## Findings that materially change the build order

### 1. Research save and monitoring consent conflict

Paper N95 assigns versioned Options case content to the existing Thesis owner. The inspected protected Thesis contract remains v1, while a substantial unpublished v2 candidate exists. Current Macro `engine/thesis_condition_monitor.py` describes active theses as subscriptions. Its `plan_enqueue` function matches engine windows by subject and produces pending outbox rows. User falsifier prose is included in the message; it is not thereby evaluated as an arbitrary custom five-minute rule.

A naive active-Thesis “Save research” implementation could therefore contradict the Paper promise that saving does not activate monitoring. **Actual runtime enablement and delivery were not inspected. This is a source/contract conflict, not a claim that notifications fired.** P08 must resolve backward-compatible consent semantics before P01 admits save writers. C10 can recover the existing candidate without activation or SQL installation.

### 2. Much of Company research already exists

MegaPane already composes the relevant financial, market, ownership and research leaves. CompanyIntelligenceV2Current already contains current-event Brief, Results, Call and Sources consumers. eventWorkspace has typed structured Q&A and source replay. An old transcript-unavailable design specimen is not evidence that the entire Q&A backend is missing. C01–C03 preserve and qualify existing behavior rather than commissioning replacement applications.

### 3. Historical knowledge is narrower than the mockups imply

`fund.ts` uses latest-filing-wins normalization, not a complete archive of superseded figures. `security_navigation.py` has real graph neighborhoods but uses latest published owner identity, explicitly sets `historical_identity_claim=false`, and retains a `research_internal_only` ceiling. Its knowledge cutoff applies to graph neighborhoods, not historical identity. A History tab or past event selection does not supply the missing historical contracts. P04/P05 resolve these within existing owners.

### 4. Manual valuation is independently buildable

The Company valuation rail `9DV-0` fully specifies a manual EPS × multiple index sensitivity and inverse hurdle. C04 can build it without live estimates. P03 separately owns company-populated valuation, consensus and alternative methods. Likewise, C06 exact expiration payoff is independent of P07 pre-expiry valuation.

### 5. Existing Options work needs continuation, not duplication

Terminal #780, #781, #686, #723/#768 and #640/#661 already carry substantial implementation. Macro #7533, #8383 and #7306 are merged source contributions, not natural runtime/consumer proof. Preserve their current owners, stacks, review gates and release holds.

### 6. Save success and status reads need precise interpretation

`terminal/lib/savedViews.ts` accepts only its supported filter fields and discards unknown fields. A successful response does not prove a complete heatmap recipe survived. An authenticated owner-scoped `/api/thesis-fire-status` read exists despite a stale comment saying no reader. Pending, deferred and sent records do not prove arbitrary evaluator support, opt-in activation or confirmed destination delivery. No matching row remains unavailable, not “open”.
