# RS × confirmed 30m pivot Terminal research consumer

Operation: `rs30-pivot-terminal-build-20261009`. Source carrier: Terminal PR #879. This is a product/replay implementation, not a scientific experiment registration or an edge verdict.

The four frozen comparison arms are RS + confirmed pivot, pivot without RS, RS + EMA20 reclaim, and EMA20 reclaim without RS. RS is a proxy: positive benchmark excess log close return over 65 and 260 aligned completed regular-session 30m candles by default. Half-days make those approximately five/twenty full sessions, not literal session lookbacks or historical cross-sectional percentiles. All arms share the same next-open fill, stop buffer, 2R target, hold and round-trip cost assumptions. Each family freezes its candidate pool before the RS filter or future outcomes; the RS/control pair shares that denominator. Different holding paths may suppress different overlapping candidates; this is disclosed, not treated as matched scientific treatment/control evidence.

Version `terminal.rs_pivot_study.v2` / engine `rs30-causal-v2` exports ET wall-clock display epochs. They are NOT Unix UTC instants. A candle's open remains its chart anchor; its close is the observation clock. A swing extreme is confirmed only at the right-two candle close and becomes a reclaim level on a later candle. Entry is the next permitted exchange candle open, including next-session/holiday/half-day transitions. An intrabar stop/target has an unknown fill instant within that candle; the exported observation is its close. At an open gap the exact open is observed. Stop beats target in a same-candle tie. MFE/MAE are conservative OHLC estimates, not certified tick paths.

The consumer fetches the incumbent `/api/intraday?tf=5m&ext=0` endpoint under the existing identity, source and rate-limit policies. It reuses `resampleUsEquitySession`, retaining only buckets with all six nominal 5m slots and a completed observation cutoff. Nominal slots do not certify feed completeness, liquidity, provider rights or PIT availability. Only fully aligned sessions enter the study. Missing scheduled exchange sessions and an ambiguous absolute log open/previous-close gap above log(1.25) split continuity and reset all EMA/ATR/RS/pivot state. This conservative discontinuity screen is NOT a corporate-action classifier. Held positions crossing an unobserved segment remain unresolved, excluded from resolved-trade metrics. Rejected risk, missed entries, RS exclusions, overlaps, unresolved and right-censored candidates remain visible. A zero-trade arm has null profitability metrics.
