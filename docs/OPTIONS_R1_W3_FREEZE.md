## W3A frozen spec — flow confidence, corrections and possible packages

### A1. Rules (binding; MASTERPLAN §7.4, FEATURE_MATRIX F13, F22, F31–F36, F41, F48, F56, F57, A07)

- Every print the user can inspect carries: event time, receipt time, exchange and condition metadata, correction/cancel lineage, price, size, premium (`contracts × price × multiplier`, multiplier shown), contract identity (underlying, expiry, strike, right) and the contemporaneous quote context when the owner has it. A clock the owner does not carry is rendered "not supplied", never filled from the receipt time.
- Side is a classification **estimate**. The inspector shows the method name, its confidence and the `unknown` / `mixed` states as first-class values. Unknown stays in every denominator: "82 % of eligible premium classified · 18 % unknown" is the shape; filters may hide unknown rows from a list but never from a total or a ratio.
- Calls versus puts (unsigned totals, separately labelled) is a different fact from estimated buy/sell direction; the two never share a column or a colour.
- Volume versus prior open interest is rendered as a dated ratio ("1.63× prior OI, OI as of <date>"); the words "opening", "closing", "bullish" and "bearish" do not appear on that fact.
- A possible package needs explicit grouping evidence (same underlying and expiry, leg identity, time delta, quantity match) and carries the label "possible" with its evidence; "confirmed" exists only when a package source is admitted (A07) — until then the inspector states that no package source is admitted. Similar timestamps alone never group prints.
- Corrected prints keep their lineage (original and corrected rows linked); cancelled prints leave totals but stay listed with their cancel receipt. Deduplication, backpressure and session-close behaviour are the flow owner's and are reused, not re-implemented.
- Ranking (F31, F48) uses explicit premium/volume units and a stable sort during updates; the summary and the full view share one source and one ranking, and a click carries the filter context.
- Activity persistence (F56, F57) is a real observation sequence with session-close behaviour and missing periods shown; no squeeze probability or "pressure score" without a validated claim (W4C).

## W3B frozen spec — comparable volatility and event context

### B1. Rules (binding; MASTERPLAN §7.5, FEATURE_MATRIX F12, F47, F51, F60–F64, A08)

- Compare like with like: a comparison names the quote side (mid), the delta convention (spot delta), the tenor, the time interpolation (variance-linear in calendar time), the rate/dividend convention and the observation clock. Interpolated and extrapolated points are labelled; a sparse or unsupported wing is never drawn through (the RP05 fixture leaves the 10Δ call empty).
- Term structure and skew render in 2D first with table access; 3D stays an optional Research Lab view.

## W3C frozen spec — condition watches and review history

### C1. Rules (binding; MASTERPLAN §4.5 and §137 region, DESIGN_SPEC "Plan → review", FEATURE_MATRIX F16, F36, A06)

- A watch condition names the metric, the instrument or contract scope, the threshold, the source and the cadence, and is one of `price_proximity`, `price_touch`, `exposure_change`, `rotation_transition`, `event_time`; a price condition and an exposure condition are different objects and are never merged.
- Evaluation, delivery, deduplication, cooldown and access control belong to the existing alerts owner; W3C adds condition kinds the owner can evaluate and nothing else. Only kinds the owner evaluates are offered.
- The frozen evaluation semantics for a price touch: eligible trade conditions are named; the session policy is explicit; arming while already above the threshold does not fire; a fresh eligible trade below the threshold re-arms; the cooldown is explicit (30 minutes in the design fixture) and shown; stale input pauses evaluation; an unseen crossing is never inferred during an observation gap; a correction re-evaluates and is recorded, never silently.
- A watch is not an order and implies no profitable signal; the words say so where the watch is created.
- A watch is linked to the saved investigation revision it was created from (W2C). The firing receipt (condition, source clock, observed value, delivery) is retained by the alerts owner and shown in review.
- Review shows what changed, what stayed supported, which condition fired, and the user's assessment — `confirmed`, `contradicted` or `inconclusive` — recorded as a new immutable revision of the same investigation through the existing command; `inconclusive` is a first-class choice, never a forced binary.

## Rulings

- Unknown is never rendered as zero: an unclassified side, an unsupported wing, a missing clock and an observation gap each say what is missing.
- No squeeze probability, no inferred causal headline, no confirmed package without an admitted package source, no confidence level on an expected move.
- The alerts owner is the only evaluator and the only transport; no second watcher, queue or cron.
- A watch is not an order; a review assessment is the user's judgement, stored as a revision, never computed.
